How to send it

Three things, and only one of them matters

The file
Zip the calibration and attach it. That single file answers most questions before they are asked.
One line
What the car does now, or what you want to know. Plain words beat a technical report.
Optional
Your read or flash tool, and any log you have. Only matters if you are about to write to the ECU.

You do not need to know any of the technical detail. No part numbers, no addresses, no table names. Send the file and ask the question. Working out what the numbers mean is our job, and it is the only thing we do.

Scope

SIM4LKE ECU or nothing

The Siemens SIM4LKE ECU only: C180K, C200K and C230K Kompressor in the Mercedes W203, CL203, S203, C209, A209 and R171.

  • SIM4LSE and SIM4LE are different ECUs with different layouts. No answer will be invented for them.
  • Anything that is not a 1 MiB SIM4LKE read is identified as such, not guessed at.
  • No remote connection to your car, and no flashing on your behalf. Ever.
  • No promise that a file is safe for an engine nobody has looked at.
  • No sharing your file, your logs or your details with anyone else.

Two files. On the house. Then you judge.
